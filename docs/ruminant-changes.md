## E. Design direction (both poultry and ruminants)
Client wants poultry and ruminant catalogues to look the same: same colors, same card style, same transitions.
Reference sites: kemin.com, cjbio.net, adisseo.com. Common pattern:
- Clean white background, one brand color for accents and links, lots of whitespace
- Category cards: image on top, title, one short line, small "Explore →" link
- Product cards: image, product name, short tagline, "Details →" link
- Simple hover: slight lift or image zoom, short fade only. No heavy motion.
- Product pages short and scannable: name + tagline + intro + image, then specs table, key benefits, applications.
