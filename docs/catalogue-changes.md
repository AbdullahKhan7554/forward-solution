## F. Fly Control: Antifly
Tagline: Poultry feed premix for fly control through larvae growth regulation
Intro: Antifly is a feed additive containing cyromazine, an insect growth regulator. About 99.9% of the dose passes through the bird and is excreted in the manure. There it stops fly larvae from developing into adult flies, which breaks the breeding cycle and reduces fly numbers in the poultry house.
Specs:
- Product: Antifly
- Category: Insect growth regulator (larvicide) premix
- Active ingredient: Cyromazine (triazine class)
- Application: Poultry feed
- Technology: Cellulose-coated, passes through the bird and acts in the manure
- Primary benefits: Fly control, disease prevention, ammonia and odour reduction
Key benefits: Controls Fly Breeding in Manure | Reduces Fly-Borne Disease Risk (Viral and Bacterial) | Reduces Ammonia and Odour in Poultry Houses | Reduces Stress Caused by Flies | Improves Egg and Manure Quality | Easy to Use and Cost Effective
Applications: Commercial Poultry Farms · Feed Manufacturers
Features:
- Cyromazine inhibits chitin synthesis in the fly exoskeleton and disrupts the hormones that regulate its growth.
- Stops eggs, larvae and pupae from developing into adult flies.
- The product is safe for poultry.
- Start early, before fly season (around March), and continue until the end of summer (around September).
- Add to feed only, and mix well.
